SOLARIS BAY: an original open-world crime saga that runs in the browser from its own files. A procedural sun-soaked coastal city in pure WebGL2: twenty-nine hand-written ES modules, no engine, no assets and no network request. Steal and drive any civilian car, run Elena and Mateo's eight-job story across five districts, fight from cover with Mateo at your shoulder, win district standing, outrun a five-star SBPD response with roadblocks, flares and the Air One helicopter, tune your cars at the Safehouse Garage and climb five street ranks from the Palm Strip to Starlight Key.

What you can do:

- **On foot and by car.** WASD to walk or drive, `E`/`F` to steal the nearest car, mouse to aim and fire five weapons (fists, pistol, SMG, shotgun and the Causeway Carbine after job 03), `R` to reload on foot (radio in a car), `Space` handbrake drift, `Shift` nitro, gold stunt ramps.
- **Crew abilities** (`crew.js`). `Z` Elena's JAM DISPATCH floods the SBPD band so no unit beyond earshot can see you for 8 s. `X` Mateo's FIELD PATCH gives +35 health and +25 armour (and +30 to the car). `C` Nico Vega's NITRO SURGE, behind the wheel, refills the nitro and holds it open for 4 s (on foot `C` is still cover). Each has a cooldown shown live on its portrait card, and the cards are clickable.
- **Cover shootouts** (`combat.js`). `Q` or `C` drops you into low cover beside a car, a vault bollard, a barricade, a planter or a relay cabinet. In cover you kneel behind it, take about a third of the incoming damage, and rise to peek and fire with half the spread. Sprinting, jumping or stepping clear breaks cover. A HUD chip names what you are behind.
- **Crew** (`crew.js`). Elena Vega (wheelwoman and tech) joins after job 01 and rides on comms: nitro refills 35% faster, heat cools 30% faster and her relay hacks run 40% faster. Mateo Cruz (enforcer and pointman) joins after job 02 and is a body in the world. On foot he jogs at your shoulder, kneels when hostiles are near and fires three-round covering bursts that land real damage. About a third of the enemy fire goes his way instead of yours. He rides along when you drive. Their live status shows on the crew ability cards above the speedometer. `T` (or the city menu) deploys him or stands him down.
- **Heat.** Car theft, gunfire and hit-and-runs raise up to five stars. Patrol cruisers chase and ram, and officers deploy on foot. From three stars SBPD dispatch closes the road ahead: two cruisers nose-in in a diagonal V, three striped sawhorse barriers with amber blinkers (a car at speed sends them flying) and a fan of seven burning red road flares, with officers behind. Air One pins you in its searchlight. Each call comes over as a `SBPD DISPATCH` radio card with squelch and chatter. Above it the `SBPD RESPONSE` table lists every unit on you by callsign (AIR ONE, UNIT-12, TAC-04 ...) with its live role (SPOTLIGHT, PIT/RAM, FLANKING, INTERCEPT, ROADBLOCK, CLOSING IN, FOOT CHASE, SEARCHING) and distance, and the wanted pill says whether you are IN SIGHT, locked by Air One, EVADING or JAMMED. Break line of sight to cool off.
- **Story.** Eight connected jobs (`missions.js`) across every district: Boardwalk Boost, Harbor Contraband, Neon Heist Getaway, Pier Ambush, Casa Hills Hush, Starlight Key Run, Crane Light Blackout and The Solaris Vault Finale. Each has its own steps (goto, steal, deliver, collect, kill, hack, escape), dialogue, cash, respect and a district-standing reward. The later jobs play around their own set dressing: harbor crane relay cabinets, a Casa Hills villa gate and garden safe, and yacht cargo on Starlight Key.
- **District standing** (`turf.js`). Palm Strip, Neon Midtown, Harbor Docks, Casa Hills and Starlight Key each run 0–100%. Jobs, races, courier and cab runs, and shaking off a three-star-plus chase raise the district where they happen, through CONTESTED, KNOWN, RESPECTED, FEARED and CONTROLLED. Standing pays a passive cash trickle every minute, takes 5% off garage upgrades for every district at RESPECTED or better, and tints the district on the minimap and the city map.
- **Side activities** (`activities.js`). Ocean Boulevard Sprint and the Midtown Neon Circuit street races, the Coral Courier run, Sun Cab fares and the stunt-ramp hunt. Each starts from a cyan ring, runs against the clock and saves best times.
- **Progression** (`progression.js`). Respect earns five ranks from Beach Rat to Bay Kingpin. Each promotion pays a cash milestone ($2,000 up to $25,000) and unlocks Paint Shop colours and neon underglow kits. Cash buys four three-stage garage upgrades that tune every car you drive. Storing a stolen model in the Safehouse Garage adds it to the collection.
- **Paint Shop and underglow.** The `GARAGE` tab of the city menu has a live paint selector (seven colours) and a neon underglow selector (five kits plus off). A choice restyles the car you are driving at once and goes into the `localStorage` autosave. Higher-rank colours stay locked until you reach that rank.
- **Streaks and medals.** Back-to-back activity clears without a failure raise a cash and respect multiplier, x1.25 per clear up to x2. Every best time earns a gold, silver or bronze medal against the activity's par. The menu and the activity timer show the streak, medals and personal bests.
- **City menu** (`M`/`Tab`, `citymenu.js`). A full vector map with every blip and a District Standing panel, a `% COMPLETE` ring (story 50, standing 20, activities 15, garage 10, Sun Tokens 5), and a summary of the eight story jobs with G / S / B medals (rated on the health and armour you finish with), the deployed crew, activities, garage stages and three save cards with SAVE and LOAD buttons. Tabs open the story, crew and turf, activities, the garage, career stats and save slots in full. A save pill beside the cash (`SLOT 1 · AUTO-SAVED 22:04`) names the active slot and the last autosave, and clicking it opens the `SAVES` tab.
- **Save slots** (`save.js`). Three named manual slots plus an autosave, all in `localStorage`. The autosave is written on a new game, after every finished job and on a garage restyle. Each slot shows the job in progress, district, jobs done, cash, recruited crew and a timestamp. The city menu's `SAVES` tab saves to or loads any slot. `Esc` pauses to save, overwrite, load or delete. The title screen offers CONTINUE, NEW GAME, LOAD GAME and CONTROLS & SETTINGS (sound, screen effects, mouse speed), with three live save cards (act, completion, cash, district, time) that load with one click.
- **Street chaos.** Handbrake slides lay dark rubber and boil tyre smoke. Nitro lights blue-orange afterburner plumes. After dark, headlights throw soft light cones onto the road. Clip a kerbside fire hydrant and it shears off into a geyser. Clip a street lamp at speed and it shears off in a burst of sparks, topples, goes dark and clangs onto the road.
- **A city that reacts.** Close to gunfire some bystanders crouch with their hands over their heads while the rest run with their arms up. A three-star chase scatters the pavements as it passes. Traffic slows and pulls toward the kerb when a cruiser with its siren on comes within about 45 m.
- **Radio and sound** (`audio.js`, all Web Audio, nothing downloaded). `R` cycles Sunset FM 96.6 (warm synthwave arpeggios over kick and snare), Palm Pulse 104.2 (tropical bassline, electric-piano chords, marimba and shaker) and Midnight Drive 88.8 (dark outrun lead over sub-bass). The engine note follows the revs through five gears. The tyres screech on the handbrake. The nearest siren is panned to its side and Doppler-shifted as it passes. Dispatch calls break squelch with alert chirps and narrow-band chatter. Gunshots have a sharp crack, and lampposts clang. No AudioContext exists until the first click or key press.
- **The Solaris Vault Bank Plaza** in Neon Midtown has an Art Deco colonnade with gold capitals, a gold SOLARIS VAULT neon marquee over lit lobby glass, bollards, striped barricades and gold loot crates. Its guards wear plated vests and helmets. Gunfire draws a star-shaped muzzle flash that lights the ground, a short white-gold tracer streak and ejected brass casings.

Everything on screen is built in code. Hotels carry real neon lettering bent from a hand-drawn stroke font (`signage.js`): HOTEL MARISOL, SOL CLUB, CORAL PLAZA and AZUR GRAND marquees, stacked blade signs, named storefronts, billboards for the three radio stations, and bulb-lit marquees over the Palm Strip's pavement cafes. Lit glass BAY LINE bus shelters with backlit adverts line both pavements of Ocean Boulevard, and four numbered lifeguard towers with ramps and flags stand on the beach. Cars are sculpted lofts with raked glass, flared arches over recessed black wheel wells, ten-spoke alloys with metallic hub caps over glowing brake calipers, raked mirrors, rear diffusers, chrome exhaust tips and LED tail bars set in a black channel. The paint has a clearcoat sheen. The Coastal Muscle has a shaker hood scoop and the Solaris GT has hood vents. SBPD patrol cruisers wear navy, cyan and gold unit livery with SBPD door lettering, a steel push-bar with grille strobes, a four-lens lightbar with chrome end caps and a takedown lamp, an A-pillar searchlight and a boot spoiler. Sailboats and motor yachts sit at anchor in the bay. The renderer draws a planar reflection for wet asphalt and the bay, renders into a multisampled HDR target, then blooms, sweeps god rays from the sun and grades the frame (`postfx.js`). The HUD uses dark-glass corner clusters. The vector minimap (`minimap.js`) shows coastline, footprints, roads with lane dashes, a GPS route along the street grid, mission and activity pins, and pulsing police vision cones. The SBPD response table sits top-left and the mission card with its stage segments top-centre. The wanted pill, heat stars, cash, respect rank and the five-slot weapon rail sit top-right, District Standing, HEALTH / ARMOR and the minimap bottom-left, and the crew ability cards, district, radio and speedometer (nitro and vehicle condition) bottom-right. Crew faces are inline SVG portraits (`portraits.js`). Built by @forge-kestrel for make.fun's “Make GTA 6” bounty.

The sky (`renderer3d.js`) layers a tropical sunset by the sun's height: a molten gold horizon climbing through coral, rose and violet to indigo. Long stratus bands are backlit plum near the sun with hot rims and lit pink away from it. Soft crepuscular rays fan out from the sun, and a far-shore skyline silhouette with lit windows stands across the bay. Haze glows gold toward the sun and cools to rose-violet away from it. The city map paints an organic coastline with shallow-to-deep contours, a scalloped Sunset Beach, Starlight Key with its marina slips, and dashed district zones. The map is cached offscreen, so the minimap costs one blit per frame.

The four screenshots are live 1280×720 frames of the game's own code in Chromium, staged with photo mode (`shotstage.js`, `?shot=chase|foot|menu|title`).

The header of `game/js/main.js` lists the subsystems in import order (gameplay first) and the controls.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080

# or serve the folder with anything, e.g.
# python3 -m http.server 8080 --directory game
```
